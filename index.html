The archive at work

Free and public. Two things are built on it.

The AnglerBook app

Every gear locker and flybox in AnglerBook is backed by this archive — brand badges, spec suggestions and fly data come from the same files you browse here.

Market Insight

The archive is the decode reference behind AnglerBook Market Insight: it is what turns a raw gear entry into comparable specifications, reported by specification and never by brand. Manufacturers who submit their lines sharpen the decoding for the whole market.
